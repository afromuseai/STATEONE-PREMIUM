CONNECT BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE TO WEBSITE ARCHITECT WORKFLOW

IMPORTANT:
Business Intelligence and Website Architect currently feel disconnected.

They must operate as:
connected layers of the same operating system.

====================================
GOAL
====

After a user generates a business idea inside Business Intelligence,
they should be able to instantly generate a website from that business intelligence context.

The website generator should inherit:

* business summary
* positioning
* target audience
* growth strategy
* brand tone
* visual direction
* industry type
* business goals
* conversion strategy

WITHOUT requiring the user to manually re-enter prompts.

====================================
UI IMPLEMENTATION
=================

Inside the Business Intelligence page:

After successful business generation,
add a top-right action button:

"Generate Website"

Button behavior:

* opens Website Architect
* automatically transfers the generated business intelligence context
* preloads the website generation workflow
* starts generation using the existing business strategy

====================================
DATA FLOW
=========

When user clicks:
Generate Website

Pass:

* business idea
* generated business summary
* market positioning
* audience
* branding direction
* style direction
* business category
* growth strategy
* chatbot ideas
* automation strategy
* website structure recommendations

into Website Architect.

====================================
IMPORTANT
=========

Do NOT only pass:
the raw business prompt.

Pass:
the FULL generated intelligence object.

Website generation should feel:
context-aware and strategically informed.

====================================
WEBSITE GENERATOR BEHAVIOR
==========================

The Website Generator should use the imported intelligence to determine:

* designVariant
* layout style
* asset direction
* typography style
* CTA structure
* section hierarchy
* conversion strategy
* visual tone

Example:

Cybersecurity SaaS →
futuristic enterprise design

Luxury fashion marketplace →
editorial luxury aesthetic

AI startup →
modern futuristic startup aesthetic

====================================
PROJECT CONTINUITY
==================

When website is generated from Business Intelligence:

Automatically:

* link website project to the originating business intelligence project
* preserve continuity between systems
* allow users to navigate back and forth

Projects should feel unified.

====================================
USER EXPERIENCE GOAL
====================

The flow should feel like:

Business Intelligence
→ strategic thinking

then

Website Architect
→ visual execution

inside one continuous operational workflow.

NOT:
two disconnected tools.

====================================
IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULE
=============================

Do NOT create duplicate projects unnecessarily.

Instead:
associate website generations with the originating business intelligence project whenever possible.

Maintain:

* project continuity
* memory continuity
* execution continuity
* design continuity

across the operating system.
