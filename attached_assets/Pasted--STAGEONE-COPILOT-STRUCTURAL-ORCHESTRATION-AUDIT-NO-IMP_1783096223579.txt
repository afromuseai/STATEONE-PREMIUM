# STAGEONE COPILOT — STRUCTURAL ORCHESTRATION AUDIT (NO IMPLEMENTATION)

## IMPORTANT

Do **NOT** begin implementing fixes.

Do **NOT** copy Website code into the other modules.

Do **NOT** rewrite any module.

This task is an **architectural audit only**.

The objective is to identify **why Website is the only module that consistently works end-to-end**, while the other modules diverge.

---

# GOAL

Treat **Website** as the reference implementation.

Determine **every architectural difference** between:

* Website
* Business Intelligence
* Chatbot
* Automation
* Orchestrator

The goal is **NOT** to make the modules visually identical.

The goal is to make every module follow **one identical orchestration lifecycle**.

---

# IMPORTANT

Do NOT compare UI.

Do NOT compare layouts.

Do NOT compare generators.

Compare only the orchestration pipeline.
