IMPORTANT:

Do NOT modify:

* authentication

* project architecture

GOAL

Build a complete internal Support Desk system for STAGEONE.

The Support Desk should allow:

* users to submit support tickets
* admins to manage tickets
* ticket conversations
* ticket status tracking
* future support operations

* status
* createdAt
* updatedAt
* assignedAdminId

* open
* in_progress
* waiting_user
* resolved
* closed

Priority:

* low
* medium
* high
* urgent

Categories:

* billing
* account
* bug
* feature_request
* technical
* other

---

support_messages

Fields:

* id
* ticketId

* senderType (user/admin)
