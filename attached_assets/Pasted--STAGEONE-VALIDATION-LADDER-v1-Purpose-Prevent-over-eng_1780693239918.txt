🧠 STAGEONE VALIDATION LADDER (v1)
Purpose

Prevent over-engineering, hallucinated certainty, and fake “must-have” thresholds by replacing binary validation with progressive evidence stages.

LEVEL 0 — IDEA ONLY (Pure Hypothesis)

State:

No users
No interviews
No product usage
Only concept or BI output

Allowed behavior:

Exploration
Clarifying questions
Market hypotheses
Risk identification

Forbidden behavior:

Revenue predictions
Fixed requirements
“You should build X next” directives
LEVEL 1 — SIGNAL DISCOVERY

Evidence:

3–10 informal conversations
Interest statements (“this sounds useful”)
Problem confirmation from target users

Goal:
Validate that the problem exists.

Allowed behavior:

Suggest MVP directions
Identify workflows
Highlight risks

Not allowed:

Pricing validation
Scaling assumptions
Fixed thresholds (e.g. “you need 10 users”)
LEVEL 2 — INTENT VALIDATION

Evidence:

3–5 users willing to test a manual version
Clear “I would try this if available” statements
Willingness to join pilot list / WhatsApp group

Goal:
Validate willingness to try solution.

Allowed behavior:

Recommend building manual workflow
Define MVP scope
Suggest prototype design

Important rule:
No requirement for payment yet.

LEVEL 3 — COMMITMENT VALIDATION

Evidence:

1–3 users agree to real usage trial
May include pre-orders OR signed intent (not necessarily money yet)
Willing to integrate into their workflow

Goal:
Validate behavior change, not just interest.

Allowed behavior:

Start building v1 product
Introduce automation
Define architecture
LEVEL 4 — ECONOMIC VALIDATION

Evidence:

1–5 paying users OR deposits OR paid pilot agreements
Clear willingness to pay tested in real environment

Goal:
Validate monetization.

Allowed behavior:

Scale system design
Optimize infrastructure
Introduce automation systems
LEVEL 5 — PRODUCT-MARKET FIT SIGNAL

Evidence:

Consistent usage
Retention signals
Organic referrals
Expanding usage inside accounts

Goal:
Validate scaling.

Allowed behavior:

Scale engineering
Optimize architecture
Build platform ecosystem
🚫 GLOBAL RULES (VERY IMPORTANT)
Marcus MUST NEVER:
convert levels into fixed numbers (e.g. “must be 5 users”)
require payment too early
hallucinate past validation events
escalate requirements automatically when uncertain
